# Early Universe Research 
## Formerly: CPT (Cosmological Perturbation Theory) Research

My 4th year dissertation is being supervised by Prof. Siri Chongchitnan (University of Warwick). It has a working title of *Cosmological Perturbation Theory: Building The Universe from Quantum Fluctuations*. 

Since starting the project, we have moved much more towards the the theory of (single-field) inflation. 

I aim to make freely available the notes I make and some draft stages of my work throughout the year. I hope this may help masters students in future years to understand the process of undertaking a dissertation.
