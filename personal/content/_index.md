# About Me

Hi, I'm Nathan. I am currently studying a MathPhys degree at the University
of Warwick. The 26/27 academic year will be my Masters year. I am eager to pursue a PhD once I have completed my course, and am currently in the application stage. 

I plan to use this website to document my academic work (both research and teaching) and ocasionally my extracurricular interests. 

### Interests

Academically, I am mainly focussed on pursuing Cosmology-related projects. 

I have a background in both data-driven/observational Cosmology (see my work on CIB tomography), as well as currently focussing on more theoretical pursuits with work on the Early Universe and Inflation (primarily, for my 4th year dissertation).

Outside of academia my interests include:

- Motorsport (British Superbikes, F1, Sim-racing)
- Classical Theatre
- Writing (Both fiction and non-fiction)
- Radio Controlled Aircraft
