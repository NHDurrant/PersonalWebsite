CIB Research

Here I aim to summarise, as well as occasionally provide updates on, my research using the Cosmic Infrared Background (CIB) to measure bias weighted star formation rate densities. This work is supervised by Dr Tom Cornish and Prof David Alonso. It was started in 2025 as part of a summer internship with Oxford Astrophysics.

International Conference of Undergraduate Research (University of Warwick)

I am pleased to announce that I have been selected to present a poster on this work at the ICUR 2026. My session takes place on the 30th September. Below you will find the poster thaat will be presented. I understand this might seem rather opaque without a presenter, so feel free to contact me to discuss any queries you may have. I would also be happy to have extended discussions with any specialists in closely adjacent fields who find this work interesting or applicable to your own work.
