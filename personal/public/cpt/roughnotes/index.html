03/08/26 (Blackboard, following Baumann's Cosmology, Chp. 6.1)

3rd August 2026

Time to start the project. I will mainly be following Baumann’s Cosmology textbook for now (see the Bibliography section, when uploaded) but my notes may include derivations the textbook doesn’t. These are to satisfy my own curiosity and oftentimes may be completely trivial to most.

In the picture above, I did not include the proof of the proposition. Although it is rather trivial, I will put that here:

We have $x^i \to \tilde x^i = x^i + \xi ^i (\eta, \vec{x}=x^k)$ Taking the total (exterior) derivative we have $$ d \tilde x^i = dx^i + d\xi^i = dx^i + \partial_\eta \xi^i d\eta + \partial_k \xi^i d\tilde x^k $$

Thus, $$ dx^i = d \tilde x^i - \partial_\eta \xi^i d\eta - \partial_k \xi^i d\tilde x^k$$ $\Box$
